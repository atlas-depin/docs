Welcome to the first Atlas DePIN development update.

Starting today, we'll be trying to publish a concise update every two or three weeks to make it easier to follow the work happening across the Atlas ecosystem.
Each post will gather the most relevant progress notes, technical changes, and links in one place.

## Developer Tooling

**TypeScript/JavaScript packages** are now available on npm: 
[`@atlasdepin/atlas.js`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@atlasdepin/atlas.js) and 
[`@atlasdepin/atlas.js-protos`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@atlasdepin/atlas.js-protos).

`atlas.js` includes wallet management, transaction signing and broadcasting, typed queries, filetree operations, and storage workflows for uploads, downloads, and subscriptions. You can find the current feature overview in the [atlas.js documentation](/docs/atlas-js/intro).  
`atlas.js-protos` provides the Atlas Protocol protobuf types and message helpers that lie beneath those integrations. 

There has also been substantial progress on the [Atlas Go SDK](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/atlas-depin/atlas-sdk/go). A preliminary release now includes a wallet manager that can sign and broadcast transactions.
It serializes simultaneous tx submissions through a per-wallet FIFO queue to avoid account-sequence conflicts; supporting both sychronous and asynchronous workflows at scale.

## Atlas Hub is ready

The **Atlas Hub** is ready for release. It will be **released once the testnet has at least two or three storage providers**. It brings the Atlas storage experience into one place: connect a wallet, purchase storage, manage files and directories, and upload files with configurable encryption and redundancy.
The hub also offers an overview of the blockchain's storage statistics.

## We need storage providers

The network needs storage providers. If you have server capacity and would like to help build Atlas, please consider running a [Cygnus](https://github.com/atlas-depin/cygnus) instance.

Storage providers who participate on testnet will receive stipends for providing storage on mainnet when the time comes.

Cygnus is the Atlas storage-provider daemon. A provider connects to the network, serves uploads and downloads, responds to proof challenges, and contributes storage capacity. The [storage-provider getting-started guide](/docs/providers/getting-started) covers installation, configuration, and registration.
